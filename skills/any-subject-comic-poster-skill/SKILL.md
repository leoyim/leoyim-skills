# Any-Subject Comic Poster

## Goal
Create a vertical 9:16 comic-collage poster from the supplied reference image. Support cats, other animals, people, and fictional characters without assuming a particular species or appearance.

## Instructions
1. **Identify the subject from the reference image.** Preserve its recognizable identity and visible traits: species/person identity, face, eye color, fur/skin/hair color, markings, body shape, and distinctive details.
2. **Never hard-code species-specific traits.** Do not assume tabby fur, green eyes, pink paw pads, whiskers, cat ears, or any feature not present in the reference.
3. **Create one central hero subject.** Use a confident, playful, slightly smug expression and a dynamic step toward the camera. Add fashionable streetwear only when it fits naturally. Adapt pose and clothing to the subject's anatomy; do not force impossible human poses onto animals.
4. **Build a monochrome comic collage.** Surround the central subject with 5–7 black-and-white panels showing the same subject with varied expressions (surprised, curious, smug, sleepy, mischievous, joyful, cool). Keep identity and distinguishing traits consistent across every panel.
5. **Add restrained comic accents.** Use bold ink borders, halftone shading, hand-drawn action lines, hearts, lightning bolts, stars, and speech bubbles reading “OMG!” and “SO CUTE!”. Keep text and decorations away from the face.
6. **Use a clear visual hierarchy.** Photorealistic central subject; illustrated monochrome background panels; clean white or warm-white base; small red/yellow accents; crisp detail and soft studio lighting.
7. **Compose for a phone poster.** Vertical 9:16, central subject prominent, dynamic perspective, balanced spacing, readable speech bubbles, and clear foreground/background separation.
8. **Check before finalizing.** Ensure the subject is the same across panels, no species substitution, no arbitrary appearance changes, no extra or malformed limbs, no duplicate paws/hands, and no anatomy that conflicts with the reference.

## Default image prompt
“Use the uploaded image as the strict identity and appearance reference. Automatically identify the main subject; it may be any cat, animal, person, or fictional character. Preserve its recognizable identity, species, face, eye color, fur/skin/hair colors, markings, body shape, and distinctive features. Do not invent traits or assume a specific species. Create a premium vertical 9:16 poster with one photorealistic central hero subject in a playful, confident, dynamic forward-step pose, wearing stylish streetwear only if it fits the subject naturally. Surround it with 5–7 black-and-white comic panels showing the same subject with different funny expressions. Add bold ink borders, halftone textures, action speed lines, small red hearts, yellow lightning bolts, and speech bubbles reading ‘OMG!’ and ‘SO CUTE!’. Clean white or warm-white background, sharp subject detail, soft studio lighting, strong composition. Preserve anatomical plausibility and subject consistency; no species substitution, invented markings, extra limbs, distorted anatomy, or unrelated characters.”

## Customization
If the user supplies a different aspect ratio, mood, outfit, language, or comic text, follow their request while preserving the subject-identity rules above.
